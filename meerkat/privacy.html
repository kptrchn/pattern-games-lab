Information We Collect

What we collect

Meerkatdoku: Logic Puzzle does not ask you for your name, email address, phone number, or a password. There is no sign-up screen.

To run the daily leaderboard and daily challenge, the app creates an anonymous account on your device and stores a player nickname, avatar choice, competition scores, and challenge times as described below. You choose the nickname yourself (or keep the random code generated on first launch). That nickname is visible to other players on the daily leaderboard.

Third-party services used in the app — including Firebase, advertising, and analytics providers — may also automatically collect certain technical information, such as device identifiers (e.g., IDFA), IP address, device type, app interactions, and approximate location, as described in their own policies.

2
Firebase

Anonymous account and cloud data

We use Google Firebase to operate online features. Firebase Authentication signs you in anonymously: a unique user ID is created for the app install. We do not receive your Apple ID, email, or other login credentials from this process.

Cloud Firestore stores: your anonymous user ID; the nickname and avatar you select; a count of first-place finishes in the daily competition; your daily cactus score for the current UTC day; and, if you play the daily challenge, your completion time for that day. Leaderboard entries (nickname, avatar, score or time) are readable as part of the public daily rankings.

Firebase is operated by Google. Data is processed on Google’s servers according to Google’s and Firebase’s privacy documentation. We do not use Firebase for email, push notifications, or Google Analytics in this app.

5
App Tracking Transparency

Your tracking choice

On iOS 14 and later, we request your permission through App Tracking Transparency before allowing tracking. You can deny permission, and the app will continue to function with non-personalized ads. Leaderboards and your anonymous Firebase account still work if you decline tracking.

6
Data Storage

On device and in the cloud

The app stores game progress locally on your device using Apple’s standard storage (UserDefaults) — for example completed levels, remaining hints, settings (sound and vibration), daily streak, and today’s cactus total. This local data is not a substitute for the cloud profile: leaderboard and challenge results are also written to Firebase as described above.

You can delete your anonymous account in the app: open Settings and tap Delete account. After you confirm, we remove your nickname, avatar, first-place count, and daily competition and challenge entries from our servers and delete the anonymous Firebase sign-in. A new anonymous account is created if you keep using the app. Campaign progress and settings on this device remain. This cannot be undone.

If you delete the app without using Delete account, local progress is removed. The previous anonymous Firebase session does not carry over to a fresh install.
